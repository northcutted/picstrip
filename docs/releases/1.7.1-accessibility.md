# PicStrip 1.7.1: VoiceOver and Larger Text

Branch `feat/accessibility-audit`. Goal: declare the **VoiceOver** and **Larger Text** App Store accessibility labels, which Apple grants when people can complete an app's common tasks with the feature. `fastlane/accessibility_declarations.json` is **unchanged**: both stay `false` until the owner completes the device pass below on iPhone and iPad.

## Recommendation

- **Larger Text: declare** after a short sighted check at the largest size (step 10). Every main screen passes Xcode's Dynamic Type and clipped-text audits at Accessibility Extra Extra Extra Large. Panels scroll instead of truncating, rows stack, and the photo keeps at least 220 pt. Camera controls keep their size like the Camera app's and show the Large Content Viewer on touch and hold.
- **VoiceOver: declare if steps 1–8 pass on a device without sighted help.** Every drag-only control has a VoiceOver path, and the audits find no unlabeled elements. Spoken flow, focus moves and announcements can only be confirmed by a person.

## Set up

- VoiceOver: Settings › Accessibility › VoiceOver, or triple-click the side button with the Accessibility Shortcut set. Swipe right/left to move, double-tap to activate. Swipe up/down on an element for its actions; turn the rotor (two-finger twist) to **Actions** or **Headings** if they are not offered.
- Larger Text: Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Larger Text › on, slider to the end. Repeat on iPad.
- Use the TestFlight build of this branch. A photo with a location (any iPhone camera photo) and a short video with a face and speech help.

## Common tasks with VoiceOver

Expected speech is in quotes. Each step should work with VoiceOver gestures only.

1. **Clean the sample photo**
   1. Home: swipe to "Try a sample", double-tap.
   2. Within seconds you hear "Scan finished. 4 regions covered." (count may differ).
   3. Swipe to "Sensitive data found. Edit redactions. … active", double-tap. Focus moves to "Redaction Regions, heading".
   4. Swipe through the regions: each reads its name, the text found, "Strong match", the risk. Swipe up/down for "Disable redaction" and "Delete redaction". The checkmark and trash buttons beside each row do the same.
   5. Double-tap "Add centered region": "Region added in the middle of the photo and selected…".
   6. Double-tap "Position & size". Each of "Horizontal position", "Vertical position", "Width", "Height" is adjustable: swipe up/down to move it by 1 %. Double-tap Done.
   7. Double-tap "Style, Solid". Choose "Blur style"; on "Strength", swipe up/down. Double-tap Done.
   8. "Undo" reverts the last change. "Done editing redactions" returns focus to the Edit Redactions row.
2. **Review a photo's metadata**: Home › "Choose photos or videos from your library", pick one photo with a location (the Photos picker is Apple's). Swipe to "Location, 4 fields", double-tap. Focus moves to "Location, 4 fields found, heading". Each field reads "Strip Latitude, switch, on". "Strip all…/Keep all Location fields" say which one is selected. Close with "Close Location details".
3. **Review, save and share a photo**: double-tap "Review & Share". Hear the summary. "Final preview" is the cleaned image. "Inspect full image" opens it, and "Reveal original" switches to the original. Choose "Export Format". Double-tap "Save as New Photo" and allow adding to Photos. You hear "Saved to Photos". "Share cleaned image" opens the share sheet.
4. **Take a photo with the live camera**: Home › "Camera: take a photo, record a video or scan a document". "Camera mode" lists Video / Photo / Document, with the current one marked selected. Point at a page with an email address. You hear "Sensitive details in view: Email Address". On "Viewfinder, Zoom 1×", swipe up/down to zoom. Its actions include "Brighter" and "Darker". Take the photo with "Take Photo" or a two-finger double-tap (Magic Tap). The photo opens in the editor and the scan is announced.
5. **Record a video**: in the camera, choose "Video". "Resolution, 4K", "Frame rate, 30 frames per second", "HDR video, On" and "Enhanced stabilization" change by double-tap. Start with "Record Video" or Magic Tap; stop with "Stop Recording" or Magic Tap. The editor opens: "Scan finished. Faces: 1. Text and codes: 0."
6. **Cover faces and objects in a video**
   1. Rotor › Headings jumps lane by lane: "Video", "Faces", "Text", "Objects", "Audio".
   2. "Timeline, 0:03 of 0:12": swipe up/down to move one second. Actions: "Zoom In", "Zoom Out".
   3. Double-tap a clip ("Face 1, 0:00 – 0:12") to select it. Then "Cover starts" / "Cover ends" are adjustable in half-second steps. "Reset Timing" restores them.
   4. In the Faces list, "Cover for face 1, Blur" opens Blur / Solid / Emoji… / Leave Visible. "Cover faces" turns all off.
   5. "Cover an Object…" › "Add a Box in the Middle" › "Position & size" (sliders as in 1.6) › "Track". The new object is listed and on the Objects lane.
7. **Bleep or mute speech**: on "Audio", swipe up/down to "Bleep a second from the playhead" (or "Mute…") and double-tap. Select the new Audio clip and widen it with "Cover starts"/"Cover ends". Each row's "Change audio edit 1" switches bleep/mute or removes it.
8. **Save the cleaned video**: "Make Cleaned Copy". You hear "Cleaned copy ready to share or save." Then "Save as New Video" ("Saved to Photos") or "Share cleaned video".
9. **Batch, Shortcuts, share sheet** (system UI around PicStrip's): pick several photos in the library picker. "Batch Process" offers switches and "Start Batch Process". Progress reads "Processing photos, 2 of 5 complete", and the pushed screen reads "Batch Complete". In Shortcuts, run "Strip Metadata from Images". In Photos › Share › "Clean with PicStrip", the extension prepares the photo; open PicStrip from its notification. With notifications off it says "Image Prepared" with Done.
10. **Larger Text (sighted)**: at the largest size, repeat 1, 3, 4 and 6. Nothing is cut off or overlapping. The editor's and the photo's controls scroll beneath the photo. In the cameras, touch and hold a top-bar button to see it large.

## What was checked where

| Task / screen | Simulator (audits + accessibility tree) | Needs a person on a device |
|---|---|---|
| Home, About, Always Cover | Audited at both sizes, light and dark | Speech order |
| Photo, metadata panel, editor, style, Position & size | Audited at both sizes; region rows, actions, sliders and focus targets in the tree | Announcements, focus moving to the editor header and metadata panel, slider steps by swipe |
| Review & Share, full preview | Audited at both sizes | Photos save prompt, share sheet |
| Viewfinder, Video mode | Audited; viewfinder element with zoom value and actions in the tree | Real camera: zoom/exposure by actions, Magic Tap, finding announcements, Camera Control |
| Video editor, Cover an Object | Audited at both sizes; lane headings, adjustable timeline and trim handles in the tree | Bleep/mute actions by swipe, preview playback (the simulator cannot play it) |
| Video scanning, batch | Batch audited; the scanning screen was checked by screenshot at both sizes | Batch end to end, long-video progress |
| Shortcuts, share extension, document scanner | Not audited (system UI or not reachable by tests) | All |

The Photos picker, share sheet, document scanner and Shortcuts are Apple's UI; VoiceOver works there as in any app.

## Audit results (before → after)

All Xcode audit types ran on every main screen, at the default size and at Accessibility XXXL, each in light and dark (the every-screen mode below), on iPhone 17 Pro with iOS 27.0. "Before" is `main` at e41a054.

| Screen | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Photo | Sharing preset hit area 14 pt. At XXXL the control panel truncated every row ("Edi t Re dac tion s", "Review &…"). Closed metadata panel text still in the tree. The photo's VoiceOver value read the raw markup "^[5 sensitive data region](inflect: true)…" in English | 44 pt target; panel scrolls, rows stack; the closed panel is removed; the value reads "5 sensitive data regions highlighted" (and a test checks it) |
| Metadata panel | "Strip All"/"Keep All" 16 pt targets, no selected state. Focus stayed on the badge | 44 pt targets with Selected trait; VoiceOver moves to the panel header |
| Editor | "Select" 18 pt and "Position & size" 18 pt targets. Each row a container of loose texts. At XXXL header, rows and Done truncated letter by letter | One stop per row with value and actions; 44 pt targets; drawer scrolls with Undo/Done pinned; focus moves in and back |
| Style sheet | Style names shrink/clip at XXXL | One column from Accessibility 3 |
| Review & Share | Header truncated at XXXL; compare hint capped over the image | Wraps; hint moves under the image |
| Video mode / Viewfinder | Resolution/frame-rate targets 17×14 pt. Top bar truncated at XXXL ("4K·…", "D"). Viewfinder hidden from VoiceOver: no zoom/exposure without pinch/drag | Full-height targets; chrome keeps its size with Large Content Viewer; viewfinder adjustable (zoom) with Brighter/Darker; Magic Tap captures |
| Video scanning | Progress bar 4 pt "hit area"; status truncated at XXXL | Progress in the status's value; scrolls and stacks |
| Video editor | Lane names hidden ("potentially inaccessible text"); at XXXL truncated beside oversized symbols. Audio hint described a drag | Lane names are headings (symbols only at accessibility sizes); hint names the actions |
| Batch, About, Always Cover | Always Cover field squeezed at XXXL | Field above Add |

**Announcements added:** photo scan finished (with count, or that checks could not finish), region added, video scan finished, cleaned copy ready, video saved. Saved-photo and live-camera announcements already existed.

**Reviewed exceptions** (in `AccessibilityAuditTests.acceptedReason`): system bar buttons and the bar title, which stop growing; the capped gesture hint over the photo; camera chrome and the 18 pt labels on the live picture; Caption 2 text the audit calls "partially" scaled that the screenshots show at full size; single-line text fields.

**Contrast** is not claimed and not failed. Findings are attached to each test run: system glass buttons, secondary/tertiary captions, white on the green accent and the risk badges. That would be the work for a Sufficient Contrast label.

## Drag-only interactions and their alternatives

| Drag | VoiceOver / Switch Control path |
|---|---|
| Draw, move, resize a photo region | "Add centered region" (button and action) + "Position & size" sliders |
| Tap an object to outline it | Same as above; a missed object can be drawn centred and positioned |
| Pinch/pan the photo | Not needed to cover anything; "Inspect full image" shows the result |
| Scrub the video timeline | Timeline is adjustable (1 s); rows seek to their face/object/finding |
| Pinch the timeline | "Zoom In"/"Zoom Out" actions; overview adjustable when zoomed |
| Trim a clip's ends | "Cover starts"/"Cover ends" adjustable (0.5 s) |
| Hold-and-drag to select sound | "Bleep/Mute a second from the playhead" actions, then trim |
| Draw/move/resize a video cover box | "Add a Box in the Middle" + "Position & size" |
| Camera pinch-zoom, tap-focus, exposure drag | Lens buttons; viewfinder adjustable zoom; Brighter/Darker actions (focus the centre); autofocus |
| Touch-and-hold to compare with the original | "Reveal original" in the full preview |

## Running the audits

`PicStripUITests/AccessibilityAuditTests` runs in the PicStripScreenshots scheme, so the CI smoke jobs on iPhone and iPad run it. By default it audits the photo, metadata, editor, style, review, Video mode and video editor screens: about 2 minutes on iPhone 17 Pro. For every main screen at both sizes in both appearances (about 7 minutes), pass `TEST_RUNNER_PICSTRIP_AUDIT_EVERY_SCREEN=1` to `xcodebuild test`, or create `/tmp/picstrip-audit-every-screen`. Each audited screen's screenshot is attached to the result.
