Share a photo with less private information attached. PicStrip helps you find and cover sensitive content and remove hidden metadata, with processing on your iPhone or iPad.

REVIEW BEFORE YOU SHARE
Scan for faces, barcodes, contact details, financial identifiers and common developer secrets. Review match strength and scan status, then decide what to cover. Automatic detection can miss details; always inspect the whole image. Name suggestions depend on Apple Intelligence and are not covered automatically.

MAKE THE COPY YOURS
Draw a box or place and resize it with accessible controls. Choose solid, crosshatch, pixelate or blur, with colors, strength and undo/redo. Solid coverage is recommended for secrets: blur and pixelation can leave details recognizable. Inspect the full cleaned image and deliberately reveal the original for comparison before sharing.

REMOVE HIDDEN DETAILS
Review location, camera, date, caption and device metadata. Choose what to remove and check the output. Export PNG, JPEG, HEIC or a supported source format, with a visible file size. Sharing presets help choose a format for photos, screenshots and documents.

START WITHOUT YOUR PHOTOS
Try the fictional built-in sample. Import from Photos, Files, paste or drag and drop; capture a photo or scan a document. Review camera captures before saving. Process multiple photos with a shared policy, or use the Share Extension to save cleaned copies or prepare an image for editing. Large images may require an explicitly chosen smaller copy.

ON-DEVICE PRIVACY
No account, ads, tracking, subscriptions or developer photo server. An optional object-selection model on supported systems may download from Apple after you agree. Importing from cloud providers, saving to a synced library, and sharing to an app you choose follow those services' settings. Protected temporary files are cleaned up as described in the privacy policy.

SHORTCUTS
The background action removes metadata only. Open PicStrip to review and redact visible content. PicStrip is open source, with verifiable build provenance for published releases.
