PicStrip 1.8 is easier to use with VoiceOver and Larger Text.

• VoiceOver: every control is labeled, and anything you would drag has another way: swipe up or down to move or resize a cover, set when a video cover starts and ends, or step along the timeline.
• PicStrip tells you when a scan finishes and how many areas it covered, and the camera tells you which sensitive details are in view.
• On the viewfinder, swipe up or down to zoom, and use its actions to make the picture brighter or darker. Take a photo, or start and stop a video, with a two-finger double-tap.
• In the video editor, each lane is a heading, so you can jump straight to faces, text, objects or audio.
• Larger Text: at the largest sizes, text wraps instead of being cut off, panels scroll, and the photo stays in view. Touch and hold a camera button to see it larger.

Automatic detection can miss details. Please review the entire photo or video before sharing.
