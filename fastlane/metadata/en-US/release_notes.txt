PicStrip 1.7 is a big update: it now cleans videos, and its camera shows what it would cover before you shoot.

• Videos: faces, text, codes and your Always Cover words are found, followed as they move, and covered — with a blur, a solid box or an emoji for each face.
• Cover any object in a video by drawing around it; PicStrip tracks it forwards and back.
• Trim every cover on a video-editor timeline, and bleep or mute what was said.
• One camera for photos, video and documents. Record in up to 4K with HDR using the controls you know from the Camera app; in Photo mode, a live viewfinder outlines what it would redact.
• Pick photos and videos together: one opens the editor, several are cleaned at once.
• Cover with an emoji, tap an object to cover it on iOS 27, and leave the last digits of a card or phone number showing.
• Always Cover: your name, a plate or your street, covered wherever PicStrip reads them.
• Scan documents, pick just your screenshots, keep a Live Photo's motion, and compare before and after.
• Sharing presets, a visible file size, and a summary of what was removed.
