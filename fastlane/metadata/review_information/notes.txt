No login, account, purchase, or credentials are needed. Start with “Try a sample” on the home screen: it generates a fictional image locally without Photos permission. Review the detected contact details, enter Edit Redactions, and choose Add Region > Add centered region to try the accessible position/size controls. Review & Share shows the cleaned output, scan status, and any findings left uncovered. Reveal original is comparison only and never changes the exported bytes.

Automatic detection is assistive, not a guarantee. Incomplete required checks stay visible and need explicit acknowledgement before interactive export. Name suggestions need Apple's on-device language model and are not redacted by default. Blur/pixelation warn that details may remain recognizable; solid is the default.

Camera permission is requested only when the Camera (Photo, Video or Document mode) is opened; microphone permission only when Video mode first records (without it, video is recorded silently). Photo mode's viewfinder analyses frames on device without storing them; a captured still gets a full scan and review. Video mode records into protected temporary storage and opens the video cleaner. Originals captured in the app are never saved to Photos automatically; closing an unsaved recording asks first. Photos add/read-write permission is requested when the chosen save mode needs it.

Videos: picking one video in “Photos & Videos”, or recording one, opens the cleaner, which finds faces, text and codes on device, tracks them, and covers them in a re-encoded copy without location, device and date metadata. Users can cover any object by drawing a box (tracked with Vision), trim covers on a timeline, and bleep or mute stretches of sound. Picking several videos cleans them as a batch with the same defaults and no review. To try it without a library video, record a few seconds in the camera's Video mode.

Share Extension (“Clean with PicStrip”; photos, videos): Process & Save makes cleaned copies; a video only loses its metadata there (frames copied, faces not covered), as the sheet says. Edit in PicStrip keeps the first original in a protected, backup-excluded app-group file for 15 minutes and posts a local notification, with no item details, that opens PicStrip when tapped (if notifications are off, the sheet says to open PicStrip). It does not launch the app through unsupported extension APIs. Imported, discarded or expired handoffs are deleted.

Shortcuts: “Strip Metadata from Images” and “Strip Metadata from Videos” are background, metadata-only actions that do not redact visible text or faces. The app-opening actions are used for interactive review.

Network: PicStrip does not upload images, videos or scan results. On iOS 27 an optional Apple object-selection model may download from Apple after explicit consent. Photo/file providers may download the user's selected item; saving to synced Photos or a chosen sharing destination follows that service's settings. Core processing runs locally.

Face handling: Apple's Vision detector produces temporary face rectangles for the current photo, video frame or camera frame, used for editable or tracked covers and local document-context assessment. PicStrip does not perform face recognition, compare or group faces, infer identity, create faceprints, biometric templates, embeddings or landmarks, train models, or use face results for analytics/advertising. Results stay in session memory and are discarded when the session ends. There are no developer servers or third-party analytics/advertising/crash-reporting SDKs. A face left uncovered or missed by detection can remain in the export. Reports contain counts and field names, not face images, coordinates, OCR text or removed metadata values.

See the privacy policy sections “On-Device Processing Only”, “Face Data”, “Camera, Microphone and Document Scanning”, “Videos and Live Photos”, “Temporary Files and Share Extension”, and “Shortcuts”.
