No login, account, purchase, or credentials are needed. Start with “Try a sample” on the home screen. It generates a fictional image locally without Photos permission. Review the detected contact details, enter Edit Redactions, and choose Add Region > Add centered region to try the accessible position/size controls. Review & Share shows the cleaned output, scan status, and any findings left uncovered. Inspect full image opens a zoomable preview; Reveal original is comparison only and never changes the exported bytes.

Automatic detection is assistive, not a guarantee. Incomplete required checks remain visible and require explicit manual-review acknowledgement before interactive export. Background visual-redaction workflows reject incomplete checks. Name suggestions are available only where Apple's on-device language model is available and are not redacted by default. Blur/pixelation warn that details may remain recognizable; solid is the default.

Camera permission is requested only after Take Photo or Scan Document. PicStrip's viewfinder analyses frames on device without storing frames or results. The live boxes are guidance; a captured still receives a full scan and review. A system camera fallback is available. Originals captured in the app are not saved to Photos automatically. Photos add/read-write permission is requested when the selected save mode needs it.

Share Extension: Process & Save makes cleaned copies. Large images require the optional smaller-copy toggle; otherwise the user can choose Edit in PicStrip. Edit prepares the first original in a protected, backup-excluded app-group file and instructs the user to open PicStrip manually within 15 minutes. It does not attempt to launch the main app through unsupported extension APIs. Imported/discarded handoffs are deleted; expired files are rejected and cleaned on access. Temporary export files are protected and cleaned according to the privacy policy.

Shortcuts: “Strip Metadata from Images” is a background, metadata-only action. It returns neutral-named files and does not redact visible text or faces. The app-opening action is used for interactive visible-content review.

Network: PicStrip does not upload images or scan results. On iOS 27 an optional Apple object-selection model may download from Apple after explicit consent. Photo/file providers may download the user's selected image; saving to synced Photos or a chosen sharing destination follows that service's settings. Core processing runs locally.

Face handling: Apple's Vision detector produces temporary face rectangles for the current photo or camera frame. These support editable redactions and local document-context assessment. PicStrip does not perform face recognition, compare faces, infer identity, create faceprints, biometric templates, embeddings or landmarks, train models, or use face results for analytics/advertising. Results remain in session memory and are discarded when the session is cleared. There are no developer servers or third-party analytics/advertising/crash-reporting SDKs. A face left uncovered by the user or missed by detection can remain in the exported image. Reports contain counts and field names, not face images, coordinates, OCR text or removed metadata values.

See the privacy policy sections “On-Device Processing Only”, “Face Data”, “Temporary Files and Share Extension”, and “Shortcuts” for retention and user-directed sharing.
